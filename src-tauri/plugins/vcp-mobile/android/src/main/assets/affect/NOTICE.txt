Evaluation
----------
The frozen stratified test split contains 624 examples and was not used for
training or hard-example augmentation. Test accuracy is 0.8862 and macro-F1 is
0.8873. These figures measure the source dataset only and are not a claim of
open-domain conversation accuracy.

The model recognizes eight user-expression categories: neutral, joy, sadness,
anger, confusion, disgust, surprise, and affection. Relationship-directed
signals such as jealousy, rejection, promises, and abandonment remain under
the separate heuristic engine. The application falls back to that engine when
the model is unavailable, invalid, or times out.
